The build on one screen

At a glance

Rangers bring no party buffs, so your damage comes from keeping your own buffs and Precision up while a held-key macro does the spam. Deadshot is the engine: once Snipe is level 12, every Snipe-chain hit takes 1 s off Deadshot's cooldown.

Skill points

Stigmas

Slots at Lv 22 / 27 / 32 / 37. Vaizel's Authority first.

Passives

Focused Eye → Hunter's Resolve → Hunter's Soul

Stat priority

Daevanion priority

  1. While levelling: Snipe and Deadshot on every board, Tempest Shot and Drill Dart on Nezekan and Zikel, plus the Combat Speed and Damage Boost core nodes.
  2. By 45: Cooldown Reduction, Gale Arrow, Focused Eye and Hunter's Resolve, Critical Damage Boost, Multi-Hit Chance, then Attack nodes.
  3. Nightmare shop crystals: Hunter's Soul, then the survival passives.
Safe to experiment. Skill and Stigma resets are free (per datamine), so nothing you pick while levelling is permanent. At 45 you reset your points and rebuild to the order on this page.
Skill points

Skills, in the order to level them

Blue = levels from skill points at 45. Striped gold = the extra levels available from Daevanion nodes for that skill (one level per node, counted from the board routes below).

Specialties

A skill's first Specialty slot opens at skill level 8, the next at 12 and 16. Highlighted = take at 45. Until a skill reaches level 20 you only have two slots, so the level-16 options replace one pick. Reaching level 16 realistically comes after 45.

Passives

Leave at level 1 (or skip)

Snare Shot and Burst Arrow are only worth it later, and Arrow Scattershot pays off at level 16 on a staggered boss. Suppressing Arrow, Explosion Trap and Defiance are filler or PvP. Explosion Trap's pull is handy for gathering packs while questing.

Stigmas

Four slots, four buttons

A slot opens at Lv 22, 27, 32 and 37. The first needs the Ascension quest that follows the first story lock. Level only the Stigmas you have slotted, taking each to 5 as you slot it and Vaizel's Authority to 10 by 45. Points come at about one a level.

After 45, with Stigma Shards: take Vaizel's Authority to 20 first. Its buff lasts 20 s and crits get a 50% chance to cut all cooldowns by 1 s. Then Bow of Blessing and Supporting Fire to 15, then either one to 20.

Situational swaps

  • Stagger checks: Arrow Storm does 50 stagger damage at any Stigma level, the most of any Ranger skill. If your party runs out of time in a stagger check, put it in your fourth slot.
  • PvP swaps: Mother Nature's Breath, Ambush Kick, Stealth, Illusory Arrow or Sealing Arrow.

Quick picks

  • Bow of Blessing vs Supporting Fire: guides disagree on which comes first. Take both, since you have the slots.
  • Explosive vs Griffon Arrow: both are area Fire hits. Explosive Arrow at Stigma level 5 drops its cooldown from 45 s to 30 s, so it's the default.

The macro

One button: the full rotation

The in-game macro loops through the hotbar slots you give it for as long as you hold the Macro key. Each slot can stack several skills, the bottom one is checked first, and anything on cooldown is skipped. That is how one held key can run every skill.

What you hold

One loop, in plain words

    Why it's built this way

      Setup, step by step

      1. Bind the Macro key. Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro. It's unbound by default. A mouse button is the usual choice, but mouse buttons don't work as the macro key in the AION 1 control mode, so pick a keyboard key there.
      2. Build the stacks on your hotbar. Skills window (K), quick-slot grid. Put each card from the map above into its own hotbar slot and stack the skills with the bottom one first. Up to four skills share a slot, which is exactly what the cards use.
      3. Build the macro. Skills window → Macro (top right). Press Add Macro once per step in the map and choose that step's hotbar slot. A new step starts as the basic attack, so change or delete it. Step 4 is the basic attack, which is Snipe.
      4. Turn off the standard delay and set 20 ms between steps. Sources disagree between 10 and 20 ms. One Ranger guide warns that 1 to 10 ms can swallow other keypresses. Start at 20 ms. At 80–100 ms ping go to 40–50 ms.
      5. Set the macro to repeat while the key is held. If a guide tells you to remove the "release Left Click" command, do that too. It lets the basic attack cancel animations while you hold Left Click.
      6. Turn on Skill Queue. Settings → Combat → Controls → General Control Mode Settings → Skill Queue. Without it, Marking Shot and charged Deadshots get eaten.
      7. Test on a training scarecrow. Hold the key for 30 seconds and watch that every skill fires. Then repeat the setup on every skill preset you use, because each preset keeps its own macro.
      Two things I couldn't confirm. First, how a macro treats Deadshot's charge. If it fires at the first, weakest stage, use the Max damage layout above. Second, how long Marking Shot's cooldown is, which decides how often step 1 re-fires it. Both show up in the first minute on a scarecrow.
      Leveling version: start with just Tempest Shot and Drill Dart in one slot and the basic attack. Add Gale Arrow at Lv 7, Deadshot at Lv 14, and your stigma slots as they unlock (Lv 22, 27, 32, 37).
      Outside macro tools: everything here uses only the in-game macro. Many guides add mouse software on top, and I haven't verified NC's global rules on that. Read NC's policy before using any of it, because bans are the risk.

        Gear & stats

        What to stack

        Stat priority

        1. Front Damage Boost: its own multiplier, so it stacks with everything else.
        2. Weapon Damage Boost: also counted separately.
        3. Critical Damage Boost ≈ Damage Boost
        4. Critical Hit only up to the cap. With your buffs up, Precision (+300) and Bow of Blessing (+200) already add 500.
        Bosses resist 30% of Smite. It mostly comes from Focused Eye, Bow of Blessing and your pet, so don't chase it. On the pet board, top Rangers favor Front Damage Boost over Smite, though sources conflict.

        Passive priority

        Focused Eye first (it counts ×1.5 under Vaizel's Authority at Stigma level 10, a source inference), then Hunter's Resolve. One Focused Eye level is worth roughly 1.2–2 Hunter's Resolve levels.

        Arcana (from 45)

        • Chalice and Mirror → Focused Eye (sources conflict on Chalice)
        • Bell → Hunter's Resolve
        • Parchment → Drill Dart, Snipe or Tempest Shot
        • Compass → Deadshot or Gale Arrow

        Weapon target

        Kromede Longbow: dropped by the Fire Temple expedition, which also drops Kromede Wings. Fire Temple, Vakron Sky Island and the other expeditions are covered in the main launch guide.

        No Global wing carries Front Damage Boost.

        Caveats

        • This build follows the Asian release's kit after its September 16 patch, adapted to the Global launch client. It includes a 10 s Precision, elemental types on Ranger skills and Deadshot's extra-damage pick. Numbers and Specialty options may differ slightly on your client, so trust what the game shows.
        • Sources disagree on a few things: Bow of Blessing vs Supporting Fire first, Explosive vs Griffon Arrow, whether core Daevanion nodes come before or after skill nodes, and Arcana Chalice. One guide mentions five Stigma slots, but the launch-client data I found shows four by Lv 45.
        • Several Specialty and Daevanion details come from datamining and community research. Cooldowns and percentages can change in patches.
        • Skill and Stigma resets are free, so test the macro on a training dummy and adjust the delay.
