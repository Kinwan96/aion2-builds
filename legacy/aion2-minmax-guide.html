Step 0

Before you press "Play"

These are Aragon's "10 essential tips" topics, plus the class meta. Most of them are one-time decisions, so make them before the servers open.

Faction & server

  • Choose Elyos or Asmodian together with your friends. Your group has to share a faction and a server.
  • Duty quests, daily dungeons and Shugo keys are shared per server. Alts only help with these if they're on your main's server.

UI & combat setup

  • There's no auto-combat. Set up one-button skill macros for your buff and opener chains, but keep dodge, interrupt and stagger ("groggy") on manual keys.
  • Adjust camera distance and aim-reticle settings early. Positioning and dodge i-frames matter a lot.
  • Plan your inventory and storage now. Leveling hands you a flood of gear, and most of it should be dismantled.

Chain your skills. The same skill key can fire different follow-ups, and every fight is about timing, not rotation-spam.

Phase 1

Leveling 1 → 45, the speed route

Kanon's rule: MSQ only, except at two ascension walls. Pick up side content along the way, because you'll clear all of it eventually anyway.

Follow the yellow MSQ

Yellow icon = main story. It carries you straight to 45. Green markers = side quests, gold icons = map-completion content.

Clear the Ascension Gauge walls (~Lv 22 and ~Lv 32)

The MSQ stops until your ascension bar hits 100%. The fastest fill is a mix of 5 sealed dungeons + side quests, done in route order as the MSQ passes through each area. Community "speed route" maps exist for each faction.

Lv 22: Krao Cave, your first Expedition

It scales to party size, so solo it. Don't open the reward cube at the end. The cube costs Odyle Energy, and you'll get far more from that energy in later dungeons. (Claiming it by accident isn't a big deal.) Lv 22 is also when Odyle Energy starts to recharge.

Gear smart while leveling

Dismantle any gear that isn't an upgrade to get Enhance Stones. Only enhance your weapon and guard. Enhancing to +5 and then dissolving the item refunds your materials.

Learn skills & the Daevanion board as you go

Skill points come from sealed dungeons, strongholds and feathers. On the Daevanion board, take the corner nodes first, then thresholds, then the middle.
Min-max trick (Kanon + Grobs): on day 1, get every alt to Lv 22 (the Krao Cave quest) before you push your main. Each character recharges its own Odyle Energy only after 22, so the sooner they're parked there, the more free energy you bank.

Horizontal content: clear it all at 45

Side quests
Press J → Regional tab. All the green quests.
Feathers
Collect them across the map and turn them in at the Monolith in your capital. They feed your Revelation Amulet. Progress is synced across your characters.
Sealed dungeons
M → Exploration → Sealed Dungeon. Every "?" on the map. Clear the ones in enemy territory too.
Strongholds
M → Exploration → Stronghold. They grant belt upgrades and plenty of power.
Some side quests ("Space-Time Rift") send you into the enemy faction's land. Rifts open every 3 hours of real time, stay open about an hour, and only let in a limited number of players, so jump in fast. Check the map for portal locations.

Weekly (Wed reset)

Duty quest rewards: only the leftmost reward is guaranteed, and the further left a reward is, the better its odds. Reroll so the good stuff lands on the left. Priority: Soul Crystals (rainbow › turquoise/yellow) → Hidden Cube Keys → anything glowing gold (Unique items).
Account strategy

What's per-server vs per-character

This split is why alts exist. Anything per-character multiplies with every alt you level. Anything per-server should always go to your main.

The alt loop: park each alt at Lv 22 → level it to 45 once your main's routine is clean → on each alt, run Krao Cave / Draupnir with its Odyle, plus Ascension Trial ×3, Nightmare, battlefields ×3 and Abyss corridors. Clear horizontal content on every character. Only feathers are synced.
Phase 2 · endgame

Item-level ladder & dungeon progression

Your main's week-1 target is 1,400 item level. That's where Vakron Sky Island becomes farmable. The MSQ alone gets you roughly 1,000–1,200, and the horizontal content covers the rest.

Faction war

Abyss & PvP, the efficient way

Flight is how you fightOfficial gameplay clip · Steam

Chaotic Lower Reshanta

The launch Abyss zone. PvE gear is the priority, and PvP is optional but rewarding. Abyss Points come from kills, corridors, PvP command scrolls and Supply Requests.

Wed & Sat

Artifact Siege → Abyss Corridors

Both factions fight over 3 artifacts. Each artifact your side holds unlocks one Abyss Corridor, a 2-minute kill-everything run for Kinah and Abyss Points. You can run corridors on your main and alts until the next siege.

Every char

Battlefields ×3 wins / week

Balanced PvP mode. Victory rewards are capped at 3 per character per week.

Main

PvP command scrolls ×20

Buy them from the command merchant at your Abyss base before the weekly reset, stack them, and clear them while farming the Abyss.

Optional

Field bosses & Supply Requests

M → Exploration → Field Boss shows spawn times, so drop in whenever you're online. Everyone gets the same Supply Requests (daily "Emergency", weekly, seasonal), so the market prices of requested materials spike. Either fill them or sell those mats for Kinah.
