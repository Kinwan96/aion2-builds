How the class works

The core loop in six pictures

The Sorcerer does damage through skill interactions. Each skill sets up another, and your MP decides whether your passives are switched on.

Your MP bar is a damage switch

Keep MP above 50% for Robe of Earth's Critical Hit, and never let it drop below 25%, where Grace of Enhancement's +20% PvE damage turns off. Flame Arrow restores MP on every cast, and Blaze restores more on a marked target.

Stigmas

Four Stigma slots

Stigmas open at Lv 22, after the Ascension quest "Opening the Way to a New Ascension". Global has 4 slots, opening at Lv 22, 27, 32 and 37. Switch the loadout for the content you're running.

Element Enhancement level 10 multiplies Robe of Flame ×1.5, and level 15 multiplies Grace of Enhancement ×1.5. That makes it the first Stigma to level, and Korean players take every damage Stigma to 20.

Hotbar & macro

One held key for the rotation (Lv 45)

The in-game macro loops through the hotbar slots you give it while you hold the Macro key. In each slot, the bottom skill is checked first, anything on cooldown is skipped, and up to 4 skills can share a slot. Skills with no cooldown (Flame Arrow, Ice Chain) sit at the top of a slot or alone in their own step, otherwise the skills above them never fire.

Hotbar: stacks read bottom → top (bottom = checked first). Green = the macro presses it. Purple = you press it.
↻ After the last step it loops back to step 1 while you hold the key

Setup

  1. Bind the Macro key. Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro. Mouse buttons don't work as the macro key in AION 1 control mode.
  2. Build the stacks. Skills window (K) → quick slots. Place each stack exactly as shown, with the bottom skill placed first.
  3. Add the steps. Skills window → Macro → Add Macro once per step, in the order shown. A new step starts as the basic attack, so change it.
  4. Delay 10 ms per step, which is what most class guides use. If keys get dropped, use 20 ms, and at 80–100 ms ping use 40–50 ms.
  5. Hellfire stays manual. It's a charge skill (3 levels). Charge it fully on packs and tap it on single targets. Korean players bind it to a mouse side button.
  6. Test on a training scarecrow for 30 s, and watch your MP. If it sinks, add one more Flame Arrow step.
Outside macro software: this setup uses only the in-game macro. Mouse-driver or AutoHotkey macros are a ban risk, so check NC's rules first.

    Stats & gear

    What to stack

    Stat priority

    Manastones & enchanting

    • Manastones: Attack, then Critical Hit, then Accuracy (bosses dodge spells without it).
    • Spellbook to +10 first, since it's the biggest single jump. Then the Guard.
    • Watch Max MP and MP regen. The rotation runs dry without them, and Robe of Earth scales with Max MP.
    • Back attacks: Korean guides stress standing behind bosses so back-attack crits land.

    Weapon & Arcana

    • Fire Temple (expedition) drops the Kromede weapon line. The spellbook icon here is from that set.
    • Arcana: aim for fire-skill-level Arcana (up to 3). Crafted rings give an extra soul-mark slot.
    • Skill levels from gear and Arcana stack on top of the boards. That's how you reach the level 16 Specialties after 45.
